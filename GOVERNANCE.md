# Governance

RuntimeXRay is currently maintained by a single maintainer (BDFL model).  
All final decisions on code, roadmap, and releases are made by the maintainer.

As the project grows, we plan to move to a more open governance structure with:
- A team of core maintainers
- Public decision-making processes
- Community roadmap discussions

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

All contributors must sign a Contributor License Agreement before their code can be merged.  
This ensures the project retains the necessary rights to license and distribute the code, including for potential commercial use.  
The CLA process is managed via [cla-assistant.io](https://cla-assistant.io) and is automatically triggered on your first pull request.
